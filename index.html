About

I am a Project Engineer delivering engineering solutions across renewable energy and transport infrastructure. I hold an MSc (Eng) in Transport Infrastructure: Design and Construction from the University of Leeds, and I am a Graduate Member of the ICE, a Member of CIHT and an APS Principal Designer (CDM 2015).

Since March 2025 at Our Footprints Limited, a UK renewable energy and infrastructure consultancy, I have worked on onshore wind and solar projects. My work covers civil design, technical due diligence, site delivery, contract administration and health and safety.

Before that, I spent three years as an Assistant Civil Engineer in India, producing pavement design calculations and structural analysis for a 15km highway scheme.
